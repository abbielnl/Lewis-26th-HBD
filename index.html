The Love Letters

Happy birthday, my love. Twenty-six years ago, the world was given someone extraordinary, though I don't think it fully realizes it yet. But I do. I see it in every small thing you do, every gentle word you speak, every quiet kindness you give so naturally. And loving you has become one of the most beautiful things I have ever known.

Lewis, there are people who enter your life quietly, and then there are people like you — the kind that feel less like an arrival and more like fate finally unfolding the way it was always meant to. Loving you feels strangely familiar, like finding a place my soul had been searching for long before we met.

I think my favorite thing about you is your eyes. Those beautiful eyes that somehow carry entire galaxies inside them. When the light catches them just right, it feels as though the stars themselves chose to rest there instead of the sky. I could spend forever looking into them and still find something new each time — softness, warmth, brilliance, kindness.

And then there is the way you listen. Truly listen. You make people feel heard in a way that is so rare now. You make me feel understood even in the moments I struggle to understand myself. Your attention feels safe, comforting… like home.

I adore the way your voice changes when you speak about something you love. The way excitement slips into your words until you sound bright and full of wonder. It's one of the purest things about you. I could listen to you talk about your passions, dreams, ideas, or the smallest random thing for hours — simply because your light wakes up everything around you.

I love your heart most of all. The way helping people comes naturally to you. The way family means everything to you. The way you love with intention and care. There is something so steady and reassuring about you, Lewis. Something that makes the world feel softer and safer.

And maybe this is silly, but I love how you tower over me, even when I wear heels and pretend I can compete. I love standing beside you and feeling protected in the gentlest way. You carry yourself with such quiet strength and kindness, such effortless grace.

As you turn twenty-six, I hope this year gives you every beautiful thing you deserve. I hope your dreams find you at the right time. I hope life is gentle with your heart. Thank you for existing. Thank you for being you. And thank you for letting me love you.

In every lifetime, it would still be you.
